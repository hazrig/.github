# Activation Greenhouse Ltd 🌱

**Product growth consultancy.** We help product teams turn sign-ups into active, retained customers.

Activation Greenhouse (AGL) provides fractional product growth leadership to startups and scale-ups. We work inside your team to find where users stall, run the experiments that fix it, and leave behind the habits and tooling to keep growing.

## What we do

- **Activation and onboarding:** map the path to first value and remove the friction on it.
- **Growth experimentation:** set up a testing cadence with clear hypotheses, metrics and decisions.
- **Product analytics:** design tracking plans and dashboards that answer real questions.
- **Retention and engagement:** build the loops that bring users back.
- **Fractional leadership:** part-time growth lead embedded in your product team.
