# Code of Conduct

Activation Greenhouse Ltd works with clients, collaborators and contractors from many backgrounds. Everyone taking part in our repositories is expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect confidentiality: client information stays within the engagement it belongs to.
- Not harass, demean or discriminate against anyone.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported to [hello@activationgreenhouse.com](mailto:hello@activationgreenhouse.com). Reports are handled privately, and we may remove access for anyone who breaches this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
